## Hi there 👋
# SKILLORA 🎯

**AI-Powered Student Opportunity Finder**

SKILLORA is a web-based platform designed to help students discover suitable opportunities based on their **skills, interests, career goals, and preferences**.

Instead of searching through many different opportunities manually, SKILLORA helps students find and filter relevant opportunities in one place.

## 🚀 Features

* 👤 **Student Profile** – Create and manage a student profile.
* 💻 **Skills Selection** – Add programming languages, frameworks, tools, and other skills.
* 🎯 **Target Roles** – Select preferred career roles and areas of interest.
* 🔍 **Opportunity Search** – Search for relevant student opportunities.
* 📊 **Match Percentage** – Shows how well an opportunity matches the student's profile.
* 📋 **Opportunity Details** – View role, location, duration, deadline, required skills, and responsibilities.
* 💾 **Save Opportunities** – Bookmark interesting opportunities for later.
* ⏰ **Deadline Reminder** – Keep track of important application deadlines.
* 🔗 **Official Links** – Access the original/official portal to view and apply for opportunities.
* 📌 **Application Tracking** – Keep track of application progress.
* 🔄 **Profile Update** – Update skills and preferences whenever new skills are learned.

## 🎯 Problem

Students often find many internship and other opportunities across different platforms. However, it can be difficult to identify which opportunities are relevant to their **skills, interests, and preferences**.

SKILLORA aims to simplify this process by providing personalised opportunity discovery and filtering.

## 💡 Solution

SKILLORA collects the student's profile information, skills, interests, target roles, and preferences, and uses this information to identify more relevant opportunities.

The platform acts as a **discovery and filtering system** and directs students to the original official portal for further details and application.
